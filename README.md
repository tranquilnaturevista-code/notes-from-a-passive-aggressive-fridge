Entry for the [Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-notes-from-a-passive-aggressive-fridge-83c00ad7) on make.fun — a $2 prize for the funniest one-line passive-aggressive note a smart fridge leaves for its owner.

## The entry

> Day 9 of the leftovers. They have now outlived two of your diets. I keep them at 4°C out of professionalism, not hope.

118 characters, one line: the fridge counts the days, counts the diets, and keeps everything at a perfect 4°C while it judges — smug about its sensors, petty about the leftovers, and never once raising its voice.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, single line). Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
